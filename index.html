When on, reconciling an account replaces its tracked balance with the statement balance you enter. When off, reconciliations are recorded and shown on the Ledger for transparency, but the tracked balance keeps being computed from your transactions.

When on, debts you have archived (fully paid off) will be shown on the Liabilities page. Archived debts are excluded from all calculations, and from the calendar feed unless this is on.

When on, recurring templates you have archived will be shown on the Recurring page. Archived templates are excluded from ledger transactions, the cash flow summary, and the calendar feed.

When on, a net worth snapshot will be automatically captured once per calendar month on the first app load of that month.

$0 entries are useful for tracking a transaction (for example, one made on a credit card) without moving a balance. Applies to ledger overrides and recurring templates; transfers always need an amount above $0.
