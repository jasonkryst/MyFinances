## New finding

### I18N-01 (Low): direct `toLocale*String` calls bypass the app locale
CLAUDE.md requires the cached formatters in `utils.js` and forbids `toLocaleDateString`/`new Intl.*` per item in render loops. Outside `utils.js` and the locale files, `grep` finds **19** direct `toLocaleDateString`/`toLocaleTimeString`/`toLocaleString` calls. Any call without an explicit app-locale argument follows the browser's locale, so a Polish UI in an English browser shows US dates (this was L3 for Health surplus dates in the last audit and is the same pattern). It is also a render-cost risk if any sits in a loop.
**Fix:** audit the 19 sites, route each through the `utils.js` formatters (add one where a shape is missing), and add a unit test that greps `src/` for new direct calls (a ratchet, like `test_sleep_ratchet.py`).
